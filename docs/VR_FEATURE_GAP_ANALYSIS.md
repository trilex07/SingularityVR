## Reference conclusions

- **Dishonored-VR:** use its evidence-led D3D9 lifecycle, one authoritative UI capture/provider, per-element anchoring, and source-level camera policy as architectural patterns. It is also UE3/D3D9 but its offsets and code are not reusable for Singularity.
- **KHARVOX:** use its separation of OpenXR haptics and adaptive triggers, runtime/focus/gameplay gates, x64 out-of-process Sense bridge, and explicit teardown behavior. Do not copy PSVR2Toolkit loader internals into the x86 proxy.
- **Overlord / general OpenXR frameworks:** use clear ownership boundaries for session, swapchain, resource generations, and frame pacing; their D3D11 assumptions do not replace the measured Singularity D3D9 path.
- Novel features (NIS/DLSS, extra trigger effects) remain lower priority until P0-P3 issues have current Steam evidence.
