# Subtitle and dialogue plan

The first batch covers menu, pause, tutorial and a few dialog markup fixtures.
Subtitles and voiced dialogue require a separate inventory pass over the
`Subtitles.int` record and the matching `*_LOC_INT.xxx` single-player packages.

The next pass will assign stable IDs, preserve line breaks and markup, compare
the ESN/FRA/ITA package structure, and produce a small subtitle sample for
review. It will not replace audio or alter subtitle timing. Speaker names,
proper nouns and technical terms follow `GLOSSARY.md`.
